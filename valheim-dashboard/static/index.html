Kills require a confirmed creature death with an identifiable player in the fatal hit. Participation counts each Viking who dealt observed damage to that creature, once per death, including the killer. Highest observed skill is personal: the highest combat skill level seen in that player's attacks since combat recording began. It is a historical high, not a full skill profile or necessarily their current level after a death. Unreported skill levels remain unknown. These records cover creatures simulated by the server; damage over time, missing attackers, or gaps in observation can leave kills unattributed or participation incomplete. Earlier combat history cannot be reconstructed.
